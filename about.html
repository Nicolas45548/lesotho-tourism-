About Lesotho

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa known for its highlands, traditional culture and natural scenery.

It is often called the Mountain Kingdom because much of the country is covered by mountains.

Why visit?

  • Beautiful mountain scenery
  • Rich Basotho culture
  • Outdoor activities
  • Historical places

Experience Basotho traditions, clothing, music and crafts.

Explore mountains, valleys, rivers, waterfalls and national parks.
